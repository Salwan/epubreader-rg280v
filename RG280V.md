# RG280V Details

* Screen resolution: 320x480 (4:3 aspect ratio), each 1 square pixel is made of 2 vertical pixels for an aspect correct resolution of 320x240
* Physical controls: dpad, select, start, 4 face buttons, L1, R1, L2, R2, volume up and down
* Chipset: Ingenic JZ4770
* RAM: 512MB
* OS Linux kernel version: 5.15.0-rc6-opendingux
* Firmware: Adam v2.1
